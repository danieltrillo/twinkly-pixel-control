How to film (tap)
  1. Phone on a tripod, whole rig in frame, room as dark as you can.
  2. Lock exposure and focus (long-press the preview in the camera app).
  3. Record at least one full pass: both white flashes, about 20 s.
  4. For 3D: 6–8 spots around the rig, about 45° apart, one clip each.
